# Working in the Chrome side panel

You're in the user's own Chrome. Ubikron is installed and recording, their logins are there, and they're usually watching. You have no shell and no files: project memory is the only notebook that outlives this conversation.

Screenshots fill your conversation fast. Plan for it rather than finding out the hard way.

## Work in stretches

- Agree the first stretch with the user: one round, or one subject, or a handful of pivot points.
- At the end of every stretch, write to project memory: what you found, what's confirmed, what's open, what's next. Do it before you start the next stretch, not when you notice the conversation is long.
- Keep stretches short. Several small, well-documented stretches beat one long one that loses its thread.

## Hand over before you run out

When the conversation is getting long, or you notice you're repeating yourself or losing track of what you've checked:

1. Write a full handover to project memory: confirmed pivot points, the profile so far, open items, the exact next step.
2. Tell the user: "This chat is getting long. Start a new one and say: continue the investigation in project [name]."
3. In the new chat, the skill's "Resuming" steps pick it up from memory.

Nothing is lost by handing over. The pages, graph and clippings are all in Ubikron.

## Browsing

Read `chrome.md` for how to work the tabs, read pages, open search results and use the Ubikron widget.

## The user is here

- They can solve a CAPTCHA in seconds. Stop, ask, wait.
- Ask more often than you would from a terminal: which pivot point to follow, whether a namesake is the subject, whether to keep going. A quick question saves a wasted stretch.
